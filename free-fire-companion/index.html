Reading the numbers

Headshots multiply base damage; helmets and vests reduce what lands. High fire-rate guns (MP40, Vector) win DPS races under 10 m, while the AWM and M82B trade speed for one-punch damage and gloo-wall breaking. Use this table to match weapon damage to your character: EP and healing characters (K, Alok, Jota) extend fights, so mid-DPS weapons benefit most.

About this app

An unofficial fan-made companion for Garena Free Fire. Browse every character with detailed sections for profile stats, special abilities and damage & skill parameters, plus a weapon damage database to fine-tune your loadouts and squad strategy.

Built to never hang on mobile

  • Single self-contained file — zero downloads, zero network calls, opens instantly even offline
  • No frameworks or heavy libraries — pure lightweight JavaScript
  • Debounced search and one-pass batched rendering
  • Only GPU-friendly animations (transform/opacity)
  • Lite mode (⚡ in the header) disables all animation on low-end phones
  • Honors your system reduce-motion setting

How to use it

  • Tap any character for stats, ability details, damage parameters and strategy tips
  • Star ★ characters to build a personal favorites shortlist
  • Filter by Active/Passive skills or roles to plan squad comps
  • Tap the dice for a random pick when you can't decide

Data & sources

Ability values are community-sourced figures at max skill level and change with patches (OB48+ era). Verify exact numbers in-game. Reference guides: SportsDunia character list · Skycoach tier list · BlueStacks character guide · GamsGO tier list.
